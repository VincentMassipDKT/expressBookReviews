ERROR CASES:

curl -X POST http://localhost:5000/customer/login -H "Content-Type: application/json"                                                   
{"error":"Error logging in"}

curl -X POST http://localhost:5000/customer/login -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"username": "John Doe", "password": "12345678"}' 
{"error":"Invalid username or password"}


SUCCESS CASES:

curl -X POST http://localhost:5000/customer/login -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"username": "John Doe", "password": "1234"}'
{"success":"User [ John Doe ] logged in successfully"}
